# Client Meeting Prep & Follow-Up Agent (External / Advisor-Facing)

## The Problem
Relationship managers prepare for and follow up on client meetings inconsistently, missing personalization and action tracking.

## Desired Outcome
An agent that pulls CRM history, recent interactions, market context, and client goals; generates a tailored prep pack; after the meeting, turns notes into prioritized actions, drafts follow-ups, and loops until items are closed or handed off.
