# Cross-Team Knowledge Synthesis & Decision Support (Internal)

## The Problem
Critical institutional knowledge is siloed across teams; decision-makers lack a living synthesis that updates as new information appears.

## Desired Outcome
A looping agent that continuously crawls internal sources (RAG), identifies emerging themes and conflicts, maintains a living knowledge graph of key decisions and rationales, and proactively briefs stakeholders while flagging areas that need human resolution.
