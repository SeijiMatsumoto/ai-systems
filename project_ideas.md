# Agentic AI Portfolio Projects

This document contains the curated set of 10 agentic systems tailored to a strong background in software engineering, finance, and operations. These systems focus on actual, high-value corporate products—both internal and external-facing—where simple LLM wrappers fail and agentic architectures (loops, human-in-the-loop, multi-agent coordination, and memory) are required.

---

## 1. Multi-Agent Research & Briefing System (External / Client-Facing)

### The Problem
Analysts and clients spend hours piecing together fragmented market, company, and regulatory information from filings, news, and internal notes.

### Desired Outcome
An agent team that autonomously plans research, retrieves and synthesizes information (RAG over proprietary + public sources), debates findings, and produces a polished, cited briefing that a human can approve or refine before delivery.

---

## 2. Continuous Portfolio Risk Sentinel (Internal)

### The Problem
Risk teams are flooded with alerts and struggle to distinguish noise from material emerging risks across positions, counterparties, and market regimes.

### Desired Outcome
A looping agent that continuously monitors data streams, maintains memory of prior risk theses, runs scenario probes, escalates only high-conviction issues, and requests human judgment (HITL) when uncertainty or policy thresholds are crossed.

---

## 3. Client Meeting Prep & Follow-Up Agent (External / Advisor-Facing)

### The Problem
Relationship managers prepare for and follow up on client meetings inconsistently, missing personalization and action tracking.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that pulls CRM history, recent interactions, market context, and client goals; generates a tailored prep pack; after the meeting, turns notes into prioritized actions, drafts follow-ups, and loops until items are closed or handed off.

---

## 4. Regulatory Change Impact Simulator (Internal / Compliance)

### The Problem
New rules arrive frequently; mapping them to existing products, processes, and controls is slow and error-prone.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that ingests regulatory text (RAG), maps obligations to the firm’s inventory, simulates downstream process and system impacts, proposes control changes, and routes high-impact items for human sign-off before any implementation.

---

## 5. Autonomous Incident Response Coordinator (Internal / Ops)

### The Problem
Production incidents require rapid triage across logs, metrics, recent changes, and on-call knowledge, often with incomplete context.

### Desired Outcome
A multi-agent system that detects anomalies, gathers evidence, hypothesizes root causes, proposes and (with HITL gates) executes remediation steps, updates runbooks, and generates a post-mortem draft.

---

## 6. Personalized Financial Wellness Coach (External / Consumer)

### The Problem
Retail users struggle to turn scattered financial data and goals into coherent, adaptive plans that evolve with life events.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that maintains long-term memory of the user’s situation, retrieves relevant knowledge, plans multi-step strategies (budgeting, debt, investing), checks in periodically, adapts when new data arrives, and surfaces decisions for user confirmation.

---

## 7. Cross-Team Knowledge Synthesis & Decision Support (Internal)

### The Problem
Critical institutional knowledge is siloed across teams; decision-makers lack a living synthesis that updates as new information appears.

### Desired Outcome
A looping agent that continuously crawls internal sources (RAG), identifies emerging themes and conflicts, maintains a living knowledge graph of key decisions and rationales, and proactively briefs stakeholders while flagging areas that need human resolution.

---

## 8. Vendor & Contract Negotiation Assistant (Internal / Procurement)

### The Problem
Negotiations drag because historical terms, market benchmarks, risk clauses, and internal preferences are hard to surface and reconcile in real time.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that retrieves prior contracts and playbooks (RAG), models negotiation scenarios, drafts clause alternatives, tracks counterparty responses, and loops with the human negotiator until terms are acceptable or escalated.

---

## 9. Adaptive Fraud Pattern Hunter (Internal / Risk)

### The Problem
Static rules and simple models lag behind evolving fraud tactics; investigators drown in false positives.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that explores transaction and behavioral data, proposes and tests new pattern hypotheses in a sandbox, maintains a memory of successful and failed detections, and only promotes high-precision patterns after human review (HITL).

---

## 10. Goal-Driven Personal Productivity Agent for Knowledge Workers (External / Employee-Facing)

### The Problem
Professionals juggle projects, meetings, and learning goals with no coherent system that plans, prioritizes, and adapts across tools.

### Desired Outcome
An agent that understands high-level goals, breaks them into plans, interacts with calendars/email/task systems, reflects on progress in loops, requests clarification or approval when needed, and continuously re-plans as priorities shift.
